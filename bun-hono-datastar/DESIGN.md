# Design

The source of truth for what the app must do. Code, tests and
`DECISIONS.md` follow from it; when they disagree with this file, this file
wins or gets changed first, with the user. Replace the placeholders below
when starting a project from this template.

## Purpose

Who uses the app and what problem it solves for them, in a few sentences.

## Users and access

Who can sign in, how, and what each of them may see and do.

## What it must do

The behaviours the app must have, one short point each, stated so a reader
can check them in the running app.

## Data

Where the data comes from, what is stored, and what happens when a source
is slow, wrong or down.

## Out of scope

What the app deliberately does not do, so nobody builds it by accident.
